Translations
------------

Japanese and Simplified Chinese are supervised by the author. Korean was
proofread by a native speaker, Hotcake. Turkish was translated by a native
speaker, DarkBloodKing (https://github.com/Darkbloodking00). Traditional
Chinese is converted from
the Simplified Chinese pack. The other packs - German, French, Spanish,
Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Hebrew, Ukrainian, Thai, Vietnamese,
Esperanto and toki pona - are provisional and waiting for a native speaker;
the language table in README.md says which is which.

A correction to any pack is welcome as a pull request, and whoever reviews a
language is named here from the release after it.
